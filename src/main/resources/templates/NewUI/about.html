Meet Gistify

AI-powered summarization to make your reading smarter, faster, and efficient.

🚀 Key Features

🧠

AI Summarization

Leverages state-of-the-art models like T5 to generate human-like summaries from complex content.

📊

Graph Visuals

See relationships between sentences with interactive D3.js visualizations.

💡

Importance Scoring

Each sentence is ranked with intelligent scoring methods like PageRank.

📝

Text Preprocessing

Lemmatization, stopword removal, and tokenization — all visualized in real-time.

⚙️ How It Works

  1. 1. Input Text

    You paste or upload a long piece of text (news, documents, articles).

  2. 2. NLP Preprocessing

    The system cleans and lemmatizes text to prepare for summarization.

  3. 3. Graph Construction

    A graph is formed where sentences are nodes, and similarity forms the edges.

  4. 4. Summarization

    The model generates an accurate and concise version of the original content.
